Calibro Support

Calibro provides body-measurement guidance, bicycle setup baseline estimates, visual adjustment instructions, and local records for multiple bikes.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm that your iPhone is running iOS 17 or later.
  • Check that each body measurement uses the reference points shown in the app.
  • Record the value currently measured on your bike before making an adjustment.
  • Make one small change at a time and reassess comfort and control.
  • Follow the bicycle and component manufacturers' instructions, torque values, and limits.

Stop riding if you feel pain, numbness, loss of control, or unusual discomfort. Ask a qualified bicycle mechanic, bike fitter, or healthcare professional when appropriate.

Privacy

Calibro has no account, ads, tracking, analytics, or developer-operated cloud storage. Measurements and bicycle records stay in the app on your device. See the Calibro Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

supportarchion@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, selected language, and the screen where the problem occurred. Do not include body measurements or other information you do not want to share.
