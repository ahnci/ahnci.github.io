Calibro Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Calibro is designed to work without an account and without sending your personal information to the developer.

Information stored on your device

Calibro lets you enter and save information such as profile name, age or age range, gender selection, body measurements, flexibility level, bicycle names and types, bicycle setup measurements, adjustment history, preferences, and ride notes. This information is stored locally inside the Calibro app container on your device.

The developer does not operate a server or cloud service that receives this information. Calibro does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, or data brokers, and does not sell or share your information for advertising.

Device backups

Your operating system may include app data in device backups or transfers according to your Apple ID, device, and backup settings. Those services are provided and controlled by Apple and the device owner. Calibro does not receive or control those backups.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual bicycle records and rider profiles inside Calibro. Deleting the app removes its local app container from the device, subject to any device backups controlled by the operating system and device owner.

External links

Calibro may contain links to research papers, bicycle manufacturers, or other third-party resources. A third party may process information under its own privacy policy after you choose to open its website. Calibro does not send your saved measurements or setup records with those links.

Children

Calibro is a general bicycle setup utility and is not designed to collect information from children. Because the developer does not receive user-entered app data, the developer cannot identify the age of a person using the app.

Changes to this policy

If Calibro's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the relevant app version is released.
